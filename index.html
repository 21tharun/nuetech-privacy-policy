1 Overview & Scope

Nuetech BT Controller is a mobile application developed and published by Nuetech Solar Systems Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us"). The app allows users to connect their Android smartphone to Nuetech solar water heater controller hardware via Bluetooth (Classic and BLE). Once connected, users can monitor real-time tank temperatures, set target temperatures, configure dual time-slot heating schedules, and register their device using a QR code scan.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, why it is needed, how it is stored, and your rights with respect to that information. By installing or using Nuetech BT Controller, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Plain-language summary: This app works entirely offline via a local Bluetooth connection. It does not collect personal information, does not require user accounts, does not send data to the internet, and does not share any data with third parties or advertisers.

2 Data We Collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically, the app never accesses or processes:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Passwords or account credentials
  • Financial or payment information
  • Contacts, messages, or call logs
  • Photos, camera, or microphone input
  • Browser history or search history
  • Device identifiers linked to personal identity (IMEI, Google Advertising ID, etc.)
  • Your physical GPS coordinates or derived location

The only data the app reads and stores locally is hardware device metadata obtained from scanning your Nuetech device's QR code and from live Bluetooth communication with that device — described fully in Section 4.

3 Permissions Explained

The following Android permissions are declared in the app. Each permission is used exclusively for the stated purpose and for no other purpose.

Permission Android API Why it is required
BLUETOOTH_SCAN API 31+ (Android 12+) Allows the app to discover nearby Nuetech Bluetooth devices (Classic and BLE). Required on modern Android.
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT API 31+ (Android 12+) Allows the app to establish and maintain a connection to a paired Nuetech controller unit.
BLUETOOTH API ≤ 30 (legacy) Provides basic Bluetooth access on Android 11 and below. Declared with maxSdkVersion="30" so it is not granted on newer Android versions.
BLUETOOTH_ADMIN API ≤ 30 (legacy) Enables Bluetooth device discovery on Android 11 and below. Also scoped to maxSdkVersion="30".
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION All supported versions Required by the Android operating system to scan for nearby Classic Bluetooth and BLE devices. This is an Android platform-level constraint — not a choice made by our app. Several OEM firmware distributions (such as MIUI and ColorOS) additionally enforce this permission for Bluetooth discovery even on Android 12+.
⚠️ We do not access, read, record, track, store, or transmit your physical location at any time. The permission is used solely as a technical prerequisite mandated by the Android Bluetooth hardware stack to initiate Bluetooth device scans.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION All supported versions Declared alongside ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION as jointly required by Android Bluetooth discovery APIs. Subject to the identical restriction — no location data is read, stored, or transmitted.

INTERNET permission: The INTERNET permission is not declared in the production release manifest. All communication is local via Bluetooth. Future versions that introduce optional cloud features will update this policy and request user consent before any internet permission is added.

4 Local Data Storage

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, analytics platform, or third party.

📜 SQLite Database — Device Registration
When you scan the QR code on your Nuetech hardware, the app saves the following device metadata to a local SQLite database (nuetech_devices.db):
  • Device ID — hardware identifier extracted from the QR payload
  • Serial Number — product serial number
  • Device Name — optional user-assigned label
  • Model — hardware model designation
  • Firmware Version — device firmware string (from QR code)
  • Hardware Version — hardware revision string (from QR code)
  • Manufacture Date — production date from QR payload
  • Raw QR Payload — full QR code string as scanned
  • Registered At — local device timestamp of registration
Only one device may be registered at a time. This data is used solely to identify and reconnect to your paired Nuetech hardware. No data leaves the device.
🌡️ Shared Preferences — App Settings
The app uses Android Shared Preferences (key-value storage) to retain lightweight UI preferences such as:
  • Whether the in-app tutorial walkthrough has been completed
  • Last-used temperature set point (°C)
  • Last-used time-slot schedule values (Slot 1 and Slot 2 start/end times)
These values are stored entirely on-device and are erased when the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared in Android Settings.
⚡ Real-Time Control Data — In-Memory Only
During an active Bluetooth session the following live values are held in memory only. They are never written to disk, logs, or transmitted to any destination:
  • Tank temperature reading (°C)
  • Heating coil ON/OFF status
  • Target (set point) temperature
  • Time-slot schedule (Slot 1 & Slot 2 start/end times)
This data exists only while the Bluetooth connection is active and is discarded when the connection ends or the app is closed.

5 Bluetooth Communication

The app communicates exclusively with Nuetech-branded hardware over a direct Bluetooth link (Classic RFCOMM or BLE GATT). All communication is point-to-point between your phone and the device — no intermediary server, relay, or cloud gateway is involved.

The app operates in Central/Client role only. It does not act as a Bluetooth peripheral and does not advertise itself to other devices in the vicinity.

Commands sent to your Nuetech device include:

  • Temperature set point (°C)
  • Time-slot schedule configuration (start and end times for Slot 1 and Slot 2)

Data received from your Nuetech device includes:

  • Tank temperature reading
  • Heating coil status (ON/OFF)
  • Acknowledgement of active schedule

During scanning, device names are filtered by the prefix nuetech (case-insensitive) so that only your Nuetech hardware is displayed. No Bluetooth scan information about other devices in your environment is retained or logged.

Nuetech BT Controller is designed for adults and intended for use by owners and operators of Nuetech solar water heater systems. The app is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no personal data of any kind is collected by this app, no special risk exists with respect to children's data. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, please contact us using the details in Section 12.

9 Data Retention

All data stored by the app resides exclusively on your device and is retained only as long as the app is installed. Specifically:

  • Device registration data (SQLite database) is retained until you remove the registered device from within the app, or until you uninstall the app.
  • App settings (Shared Preferences) are retained until the app is uninstalled or app data is cleared via Android system settings (Settings → Apps → Nuetech BT Controller → Clear Data).
  • In-session Bluetooth data (in-memory) is discarded immediately when the Bluetooth session ends or the app is closed.

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Because we hold no copies of your data, we have no ability to remotely delete data on your behalf.

10 Security

Because this app operates entirely offline and transmits no personal data to any remote server, it is not exposed to the most prevalent data security risks such as server-side breaches or network-level interception of personal information.

Security measures in place include:

  • All communication is via local Bluetooth — no data traverses the internet.
  • No user credentials or sensitive personal data are ever requested or stored.
  • The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary for core functionality.
  • Device metadata stored locally is limited to non-sensitive hardware identifiers.
  • The app uses Android's built-in SQLite storage, which is sandboxed and inaccessible to other apps.

We recommend keeping your Android device up to date and enabling Android's built-in security features (screen lock, full-disk encryption) to protect locally stored data.

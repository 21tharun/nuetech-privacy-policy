Privacy Policy

Nuetech Controller  ·  Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Nuetech Controller is a mobile application developed by Nuetech Solar Systems Pvt. Ltd. It connects your smartphone to the Nuetech solar water heater controller using Bluetooth, allowing users to configure heating schedules, set temperature targets, and sync timer slots with the controller for efficient and automated water heating.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using the Nuetech Controller application.

2. Data Collection

This app does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. No user account or registration is required. The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, messages, financial information, or device identifiers linked to personal identity.

3. Bluetooth Communication

The app uses Bluetooth exclusively as a Central/Client — it scans for and connects to the Nuetech controller. It does not advertise or act as a Bluetooth peripheral.

Bluetooth is used to:

  • Discover nearby Nuetech controller devices
  • Connect to the controller hardware
  • Send commands — temperature set point, slot scheduling configuration
  • Receive data — tank temperature, coil status, device operating state

All communication is directly between the phone and the hardware. No data is sent to external servers.

4. Local Device Data

The app may store limited configuration data locally on the device — such as previously connected device identifiers, saved temperature settings, and slot scheduler configurations. This data never leaves the device.

5. Location Permission

The app requests location permission on devices running Android 11 and below (API level ≤ 30). This is a mandatory Android operating system requirement for apps that perform Bluetooth device scanning on these older versions of Android.

⚠️ The location permission is used solely to allow Bluetooth scanning on older Android devices. The app does not access, read, record, store, or transmit the device's physical location at any time.

On Android 12 and above (API level 31+), the app uses the BLUETOOTH_SCAN permission with the neverForLocation flag. This explicitly tells Android that location is not derived from Bluetooth scan results, and no location prompt is shown on these devices.

In summary:

  • Android 12+ — No location permission requested or used
  • Android 11 and below — Location permission requested by Android OS requirement for BLE scanning only, not used for any location purpose

6. Internet Connectivity

The app does not require internet connectivity. The INTERNET permission is not declared in the release build. All communication is local via Bluetooth.

7. Data Sharing

The app does not share data with third parties. It has no advertising networks, analytics services, or tracking systems. No user information is sold, rented, or shared.

8. Security

As the app does not collect or transmit personal data, risks associated with remote data storage do not apply. All communication is local between the phone and the connected hardware.

9. Children's Privacy

This app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements. The "Last Updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
